Frequently Asked Questions📌

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time with no extra fees. Your plan stays active until the end of the month you paid for.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?

Absolutely. You can switch between plans at any time from your professional dashboard. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing month.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! All new professionals get a 14-day free trial on the Standard plan so you can explore the platform before committing to a subscription.
